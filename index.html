Game Information

About the Game

Star Jump is a platform game where you control a star jumping between cosmic platforms while avoiding the rising cosmic dust. Reach for the stars and escape to space!

How to Play

  • Use LEFT and RIGHT arrow keys or A and D to move
  • Hold DOWN or SPACE to crouch and charge your jump
  • Release to jump higher based on charge time
  • Automatically jump when landing on platforms
  • Avoid the rising cosmic dust at all costs
  • Reach the highest altitude possible

Skins System

Unlock new star appearances by reaching certain altitudes or using special codes:

  • Basic skins: Unlock by reaching specific altitudes
  • Special Amiibo skins: Unlock with codes
  • Premium skins: Unlock at higher altitudes (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Void, Negative, Angelical, Demonic, Squid)
  • New Power, Virtual, Possesed and Astral skins with multiple recolors
